How medical thermography works

Contact-free and emitting nothing, in four steps.

  1. 1

    Body radiation

    The skin surface emits infrared in proportion to its temperature.

  2. 2

    Lens and detector

    A germanium lens focuses the radiation onto an array of thermal detectors.

  3. 3

    Temperature map

    Software converts each point into a temperature and colour and records the thermogram.

  4. 4

    Physician review

    The image and data go to the physician, who makes any diagnosis.
