About

The medical department of Fanavaran Madoon Ghermez Co., Ltd. We have focused on medical equipment since 2004.

1

From infrared to medicine

Fanavaran Madoon Ghermez is a specialist supplier of inspection and condition-monitoring equipment for the power and energy sector, from thermography cameras to cable fault location.

The medical department started in 2004 on that same thermal-imaging expertise; medical thermography is its main line today.

Our customers are hospitals, medical centres, R&D centres and physicians.
