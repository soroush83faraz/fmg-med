Clinical thermography cameras

Long-wave thermal camera for body-surface imaging, connected to a computer.

1

Clinical thermography cameras

The human body emits mostly in the long-wave infrared band (about 7 to 14 µm); clinical cameras work in that band and record without contact.

The camera is usually mounted on a stand and connected to a computer and analysis software so imaging is repeatable under the same conditions.
